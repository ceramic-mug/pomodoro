# Privacy Policy — Pomodoro

_Last updated: 30 July 2026_

Pomodoro does not collect, transmit, or share any data about you.

## What the app stores

The app saves three things on your device, using Apple's standard app preferences storage:

- the date your current timer session started, so the countdown stays accurate if the app is closed or your phone restarts
- whether the timer is running or paused
- your three settings (quiet mode, alert sound, keep screen awake)

That's the entire contents. It stays on your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the app.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, or email address
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers
- No network requests of any kind — the app has no server component
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, health data, or files

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Notifications** — used only to alert you when a focus or break period ends. The alerts are generated on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
- **Live Activities** — used only to display the running timer on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and in StandBy.

Both are optional. Declining them leaves the timer fully functional while the app is open.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
